Classroom Seating Plan Generator

A self-contained, zero-dependency browser web application (seating_plan_generator.html) for creating, customizing, and exporting random classroom seating plans complete with student names and portraits.

Getting Started

  1. Save photographs.xlsx to your computer from SchoolBase:
    1. Select students → Actions → Reports → Photographs.
    2. Save as .xslx.
  2. Start experimenting: Click Import Excel (.xlsx), or drag and drop a .xlsx file onto the dropzone in the left sidebar.
  3. Create room On the top row, input the layout of your room with the tables as wither rows of 2 person desks or 4 or 6 desks.

Features

  • PhotoShot .xlsx Import with Photo Extraction Imports .xlsx spreadsheets containing anchored student images, form/group labels, and student names directly in the browser without external libraries or server uploads.
  • Randomized Demo Class Generates a fresh set of randomized students with generated portraits so you can test and experiment immediately.
  • Flexible Desk & Row Configuration
    • Defaults to 3 rows of 4 two-seater desks (24 seats).
    • Adjust Rows and Desks / Row independently.
    • Switch between 2-seater desks and combined 4-seater or 6-seater rectangular desk arrangements built from 2-seater units.
    • Choose between Fill Front Rows First or Spread Evenly Across Desks when randomizing, and toggle Board Position between top and bottom.
  • Interactive Seating Adjustments
    • Drag and drop student cards between seats or move a student into the Unseated Pool.
    • Click two seats to swap students quickly.
    • Mark students Present or Absent in the sidebar roster, or add students manually.
  • Excel (.xlsx) Export & Print
    • Exports a multi-sheet .xlsx workbook featuring a visual Seating Plan grid with embedded student photos and a tabular Seat List summary.
    • Includes a Print view formatted for printing or saving as a PDF.

Exporting & Sharing

  • Click Export Excel (.xlsx) in the top toolbar to download an .xlsx workbook containing your current seating layout, student names, form/group tags, and embedded photos.
  • Click Print to open the print dialog with navigation and editing controls hidden.
